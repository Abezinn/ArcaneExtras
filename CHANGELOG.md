## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-03

### Added

- Added Heavyweight enchantment.
  - Prevents Levitation from being applied while equiped on boots.
  - Removes an active Levitation effect when Heavyweight boots are equiped.
  - Can be obtained from the enchantment table.
  - Can be found in End City treasure chests.
