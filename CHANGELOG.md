## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-06

### Added

- Added the Scourge of the Renegades enchantment.
    - Deals extra damage to members of the Illager faction.
        - Pillagers, Evokers, Vindicators, Ravagers, Witches and Vexes.
    - Has 5 levels.
    - Deals 2.5 extra damage per level.
    - Can be obtained from the enchanting table.
    - Can be found in vanilla random loot.
    - Can be offered as an enchanted book by Librarian villagers.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-06

### Added

- Added the Heavyweight enchantment.
    - Prevents Levitation from being applied while equipped on boots.
    - Removes an active Levitation effect when Heavyweight boots are equipped.
    - Can be obtained from the enchanting table.
    - Can be found in End City treasure chests.
